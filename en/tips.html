Tips

This is tips for tourists that are going to see Komodo Dragon (Taman Nasional Komodo)

Preparation

Before visiting Komodo National Park, tourists need to understand the conditions of the area, such as hot weather, hilly terrain, and limited access. Make sure to prepare properly by being in good physical condition and bringing personal equipment such as sunscreen to ensure confort during the visit.

Safety

Komodo dragons are wild and dangerous predators. Therefore, if you plan to visit, always make sure to follow an official guide or ranger and keep a minimum distance of 5–7 meters from the Komodo dragons. Do not attempt to approach, touch, or feed them, as they can attack suddenly. Komodo dragons have a very sharp sense of smell for blood, so it is important to inform the guide if necessary.

Respect

As a tourists, it is important to practice good ethics while visiting by being friendly to others and not littering. Respect the local culture and traditions by wearing modest clothing, and you can help the local community by purchasing local products such as handicrafts around the tourists area.
